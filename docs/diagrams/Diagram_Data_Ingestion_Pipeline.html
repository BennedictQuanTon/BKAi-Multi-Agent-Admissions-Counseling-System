Why reorganise (v4 → v5)
  • Numbers moved to SQL — wide CSV rows with 5 score columns made the LLM pick the wrong year (v4 C2 bug).
  • One fact, one source — tables duplicated in tonghop.md were dropped.
  • Outdated policy removed — v4 said “IELTS ≥ 5.0 quy đổi”; 2026 rule is ≥ 6.0.
  • 2026 cut-offs added — published 09/08/2026, missing in v4.
  • Provenance on every row — source_url, fetched_at, legacy flag.
